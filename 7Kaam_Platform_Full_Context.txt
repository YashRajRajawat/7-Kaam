1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & PRODUCT ARCHITECTURE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7 Kaam is a blue-collar worker ecosystem that enables skilled trade workers (electricians, plumbers, carpenters, AC technicians, painters, welders) to get certified through a 3-signal assessment pipeline and connect directly with nearby customers without booking commission fees or middleman friction.

Key Product Rules:
1. NO IN-APP BOOKING ENGINE: Customers search, filter, and inspect verified scores, then directly contact workers via Call ("tel:+91...") or WhatsApp.
2. 3-SIGNAL SCORING PIPELINE (0 - 100):
   - Video Assessment Score (AI Vision / Admin Manual Video Evaluation)
   - Trade Test Score (Interactive MCQs & Practical Tests via Groq LLaMA 3 / AI)
   - Work History Score (Verified client and contractor past work entries)
3. KAAMCARD ISSUANCE:
   - When workers register and complete tests, their score is immediately LIVE for customer contact.
   - Admins evaluate video demonstrations, assign manual marks (0-100), and issue official digital KaamCards with tamper-proof QR verification tokens.
   - Admins can suspend workers or revoke KaamCards if complaints arise.

C. WORKER ASSESSMENT & SCORING PIPELINE
- POST /api/v1/workers/:id/score-video
  Body    : { "score": 85, "notes": "Excellent practical demonstration" }
  Response: Updates worker.videoScore, computes finalScore, and returns updated worker.

- POST /api/v1/workers/:id/compute-score
  Response: Calculates composite final score (0.40 * video + 0.35 * test + 0.25 * workHistory) and assigns Tier (EXPERT / GOLD / SILVER / BRONZE).

- POST /api/v1/workers/:id/issue-kaamcard
  Response: Generates digital KaamCard with unique QR Token and PDF download link.

- POST /api/v1/workers/:id/add-work-history
  Body    : { "employerName": "Sobha Developers", "role": "Electrician", "rating": 5, "verified": true }
  Response: Adds past work entry and recomputes workHistoryScore.

D. PUBLIC QR VERIFICATION
- GET /api/v1/verify/:qrToken
  Response: Public verification page details showing card authenticity, worker name, trade, score, tier, and status.
